Privacy Policy · Last updated July 2026

Welcome to Nevilete, an AI-powered CFA study tracker. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your data.

Information We Collect

When you create an account, we may collect your email address, name, and profile picture if you sign in with Google. We also store study sessions, goals, progress, streaks, curriculum completion, and other study-related information so your progress syncs across your devices.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to provide core Nevilete features, including tracking study sessions, generating analytics, maintaining progress, personalizing your experience, and improving the platform. We do not sell your personal information.

AI Features

Nevilete may use AI models to generate study insights, summaries, and personalized recommendations. Only the information necessary to provide these features is processed.

Data Storage

Your data is securely stored using Supabase. Reasonable security measures are implemented to protect your information. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services including Google for authentication and Supabase for authentication and database services. Their respective privacy policies also apply.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us.
